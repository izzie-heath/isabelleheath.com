the idea

A place where good habits grow

For anyone who feels forgetful, unmotivated, or disorganized, HABITat turns a daily to-do list into a game. You check off your habits each day, build up streaks, and see how your consistency stacks up against everyone else’s.

Daily habits

Add the habits you want to keep, such as going to the gym or drinking water, and check them off each day. They reset every morning.

Streaks

Finish a habit on consecutive days and its streak grows. Miss a day and it starts over, so consistency is what counts.

Leaderboard

Every user’s total streak is ranked on a shared leaderboard, so you can see how you compare.

Live updates

When someone checks off a habit, the leaderboard re-sorts for everyone with the page open. No refresh needed.

Quote of the day

The dashboard greets you with a motivational quote pulled from a third-party quotes service.

Accounts

People register and log in, and each person’s habits are stored privately under their account.

under the hood

What happens when you check off a habit

The full stack is mine, from the button you click to the database it writes to.

  1. 01

    The app updates

    React marks the habit done. If it was finished yesterday the streak grows by one, otherwise it restarts at one.

  2. 02

    The server saves it

    The app sends the change to my Express API, which checks your login cookie and stores the habit in MongoDB.

  3. 03

    Streaks get totaled

    The server adds up all of your habits’ streaks into a single score for the leaderboard.

  4. 04

    Everyone sees it

    The new score is pushed over a WebSocket to every open leaderboard, which re-sorts instantly.

Passwords are hashed with bcrypt before they’re stored, and each login sets a secure, HTTP-only cookie. I deployed the app to an AWS server with its own domain name and HTTPS.
