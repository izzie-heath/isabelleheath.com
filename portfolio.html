how I work

My design process

  1. 01

    Research

    Understand the people, the problem, and the constraints before opening Figma.

  2. 02

    Wireframe

    Sketch low-fidelity flows to test structure and logic early and cheaply.

  3. 03

    Prototype

    Build high-fidelity, interactive Figma prototypes ready for real feedback.

  4. 04

    Build & test

    Bring the design to life in React and iterate based on how it’s used.
